# ZIC Ordinary Life Policies — User Guide

## Purpose

The Ordinary Life (OL) Policies module is the operational record for an issued insurance contract. A policy is created from an eligible proposal only after the first premium commitment has been fully posted. After issuance, the policy snapshot is the authoritative source for servicing, documents, lifecycle processing, and downstream integrations.

> **Contract principle:** upstream quotation and proposal records may change, but an issued policy retains the agreed terms captured at issuance. Material changes are made through an endorsement and are never silently overwritten.

## Finding a policy

Open **Ordinary Life → Policies**. The table is designed for daily work-queue use and supports policy number, policyholder, national identity number, telephone, status, product, agent, currency, commencement-date, and maturity-date searches or filters. The detail page provides the contract header, snapshot sections, linked proposal and commitments, member/rider/benefit data, generated documents, and audit history.

| Common task | Where to work | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Find a contract | Policy list search and filters | A table row with status, holder, product, premium, dates, and permitted actions |
| Review the agreed contract | Policy detail → Overview | The issuance snapshot and linked source proposal |
| Review servicing history | Policy detail → Endorsements / Audit | Before/after values, reason, actor, source channel, and effective date |
| Print contract material | Policy detail → Documents or Print | A branded contract or schedule generated by the unified document engine |
| Review operational totals | Policy dashboard → KPIs | Active, lapsed, newly issued, maturing-soon, and value measures |

## Issuance

Policy issuance is normally initiated from an approved proposal. The proposal must be in `AWAITING_FIRST_PREMIUM` or `PAYMENT_READY`, must contain a selected plan, and must have a linked first-premium commitment with `COMPLETED` status, zero balance, and full funding. If a proposal is already converted, retrying the issuance action returns the existing policy rather than creating a duplicate.

Before issuing, verify the policyholder, selected plan, premium frequency, term, sum assured, members, riders, benefits, and first-premium payment. The issuance operation copies the selected contract data and records a `PolicyIssued` event and audit row.

## Servicing and endorsements

Use an endorsement for any material post-issuance change. The module supports premium changes, term changes, member additions or removals, beneficiary changes, address changes, and other configured endorsement types. An endorsement records the effective date, description, reason, source channel, actor, and immutable before/after snapshots.

Premium changes are checked against the configured endorsement rating band. Member changes respect the minimum and maximum member limits captured in the policy snapshot. Removing a member ends the active history rather than deleting the record. Endorsements are not available for cancelled, expired, or lapsed contracts until the relevant reinstatement path has been completed.

## Lifecycle operations

Lifecycle jobs are rule-based and use effective-dated OL Parameters. Unpaid policy commitments are evaluated against the configured grace envelope. A policy may move from `ACTIVE` to `LAPSED`, and an eligible lapsed policy may return to `ACTIVE` after payment of the required premium, interest, and penalty within the reinstatement window. A policy that reaches its maturity date without a maturity action can move to `EXPIRED`.

| Status | Meaning | Typical next action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `ACTIVE` | Contract is in force | Service, endorse, loan, withdraw, lapse evaluation, or maturity evaluation |
| `LAPSED` | Premium obligations were not settled within the grace envelope | Reinstate or convert to paid-up when configured |
| `PAID_UP` | Future premiums are stopped and reduced cover continues | Service within permitted rules or await maturity |
| `SURRENDER_PENDING` | Surrender valuation and payout requisition have been created | Finance/front-office settles the requisition |
| `SURRENDERED` | Surrender payout has been settled | Terminal contract record |
| `MATURED_PENDING_PAYMENT` | Maturity claim is created and awaits approval or payment | Verify, approve, and pay the maturity claim |
| `MATURED` | Maturity payout is complete | Terminal contract record |
| `EXPIRED` | Contract reached maturity without a maturity action | Review or reopen only through an authorized business process |
| `CANCELLED` | Contract was cancelled, including a free-look cancellation | Terminal contract record |
| `CLAIM_SETTLED` | An exhausting claim was applied | Claims team owns the downstream case |
| `TERMINATED` | Contract has ended through a terminal business event | Retain for audit and reporting |

## Loans, withdrawals, surrender, and cancellation

A policy loan is available only where the product and effective-dated loan setup permit it. The request is checked against cash value and configured percentage and amount limits. Approved loans are disbursed through a payment requisition. Repayments apply interest before principal and maintain a `last_interest_date` so a same-day retry does not double-accrue interest.

Withdrawals are checked against cash value, outstanding loans, prior withdrawals, and product flags. Surrender calculates the configured value, deducts applicable charges and outstanding loan balances, and creates a payment requisition. The policy remains `SURRENDER_PENDING` until that payout is settled by the payment-owning process. Cancellation requires a reason; a cancellation inside the configured free-look period creates the appropriate refund requisition.

## Maturity

The maturity process creates a claim-like `MaturityClaim`, calculates the maturity value from the policy snapshot, deducts active loan balances, and creates a payment requisition when the configured gates are satisfied. Required documents and approval are enforced before payment. A successful payment moves the policy to `MATURED` and records a payment reference.

## Documents and audit

Contract and schedule PDFs are generated through the unified documents engine. The generated instance retains the source policy, template name and version, generator, timestamp, page count, checksum, and correlation information. Use the Documents tab to preview or download the current document while retaining prior versions.

Every state change and material servicing action must show an actor (or the batch/system source), before and after state, reason, source channel, and correlation identifier when available. If an action is blocked, follow the resolution steps in the structured error message instead of editing database values directly.

## Demo data

A release-safe demonstration dataset can be created with:

```bash
python manage.py seed_ol_policy_scenarios
```

The command is additive and idempotent. It creates or reuses eight policies covering active, lapsed, reinstated, surrendered, matured, active-loan, cancelled, and paid-up scenarios. It also reports proofs for the BR-03 missing-first-premium guard, an outside-window reinstatement, and an ineligible first-year surrender.
