# OL Maturity Installments — User Guide

This guide is for ZIC policyholders whose matured Ordinary Life policy is being
paid out in installments instead of a single lump sum.

## 1. What is a maturity installment plan?

When your Ordinary Life policy matures, ZIC pays out the maturity value. If your
payout is set up as installments, that value is paid to you in a series of
payments over a fixed term — for example, ten annual payments. Each payment is
called an **installment**, and the full set is called your **installment plan**.

The plan is generated from the product's published installment rates, so the sum
of all installments always equals your maturity value (any remainder from
rounding is distributed so the total matches exactly).

## 2. Viewing your plan

You can view your installment plan through the policyholder portal. The portal
shows:

- your plan number and the linked policy number;
- the plan status and currency;
- how many installments you have paid and how many are due;
- the full schedule of installments with due dates and amounts.

The portal is read-only and scoped to you — you can only ever see your own
plans.

## 3. Plan statuses you may see

| Status | What it means |
|--------|---------------|
| `Created` | Your plan is set up; the first installment is not yet due. |
| `Active` | Payments have started; your plan is running normally. |
| `Completed` | Every installment has been paid; the plan is finished. |
| `Cancelled` | The plan was closed; remaining installments were waived. |

An individual installment can be `Scheduled`, `Payment pending`, `Paid`,
`Missed`, or `Waived`.

## 4. How you receive payments

1. When an installment becomes due, ZIC raises a disbursement against the bank
   account you have registered as your **primary, verified** account.
2. Once the payment is confirmed, the installment is marked **Paid** and your
   plan moves forward.
3. When the final installment is paid, your plan is marked **Completed**.

Keep your bank account details current with ZIC — if no valid bank account is on
record, an installment cannot be disbursed.

## 5. Missed installments

If an installment is not paid by its due date, it is flagged as **Missed** and
ZIC will notify you. You can still make the payment — a missed installment
remains payable. Contact ZIC if you need help bringing your plan back on track.

## 6. Corrections and reversals

If a payment was made to you in error, ZIC may reverse it within a short
window (by default 7 days from payment). After a reversal the installment is
re-scheduled, and ZIC may re-issue it once any correction is complete.

## 7. Frequently asked questions

**My plan shows "Cancelled" — what happened?**
ZIC closed the plan before it was fully paid. Remaining installments were
waived, so nothing further is payable. Contact ZIC if you did not request this.

**Why was an installment not paid on time?**
The most common causes are a missing or unverified bank account, or a payment
that was reversed. Check your bank details on file and contact ZIC if you believe
the payment should have been made.

**Can I see a copy of my schedule?**
Yes — ZIC can print a maturity schedule or a payment advice for your plan. These
documents show your full installment plan and payment history.

## 8. Getting help

Contact ZIC policy administration for anything in this guide. When you write in,
quote your plan number (format `MIP-YYYYMMDD-XXXXXXXXXX`) and your policy number
so the team can locate your plan quickly.
